Why Roomie

Sharing a space is personal.

Rent and location matter. But so do the everyday things that make a place feel comfortable — or make it difficult to live in.

🌙

Different daily rhythms

Sleep schedules, morning routines and quiet hours can shape how comfortable a shared home feels.

🧹

Different home habits

Cleanliness, shared chores and how people use common spaces can become sources of tension.

📚

Different priorities

Study time, noise, guests and spending expectations all matter when people live together.
